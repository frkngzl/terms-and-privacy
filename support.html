Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI work?

Alters.ai uses Google Gemini AI technology to provide real-time mentorship. Your messages are processed instantly and are not stored permanently.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. We have a Zero-Persistence policy. Conversations are deleted the moment you end the chat session.

How can I cancel my subscription?

All subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can cancel via: iPhone Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > Alters AI > Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

All refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a refund request.

Account & Data Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data:

  • In the App: Go to Settings > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data.
  • Via Email: If you cannot access the app, send a request to thatsmyjobemailf@gmail.com with your User ID. We will process your request within 30 days.

Important: Deleting your account does not cancel active subscriptions. Please cancel your subscription via iPhone Settings before deleting your account.
