Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI work?

Alters.ai uses Google Gemini AI technology to provide real-time mentorship. Your messages are processed instantly and are not stored permanently.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. We have a Zero-Persistence policy. Conversations are deleted the moment you end the chat session.

How can I cancel my subscription?

All subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can cancel via: iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Account & Data Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, go to the Settings menu within the Alters.ai app and select "Delete Account". If you cannot access the app, please email us with your User ID.
