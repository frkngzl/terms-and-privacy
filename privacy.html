Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Alters.ai ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application in compliance with the GDPR and KVKK.

1. Data Controller

The data controller for Alters.ai is the Alters.ai Team. For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at:

thatsmyjobemailf@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We prioritize data minimization. We collect the following:

  • Authentication: Anonymous Login (Device ID) and Sign in with Apple.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous interactions via Firebase Analytics.
  • Subscriptions: Transaction IDs via Adapty and Apple (No credit card data).
  • Advertising: Advertising ID (IDFA) via Google AdMob (subject to consent).

3. Zero-Persistence Messaging Policy

  • No Storage: Your messages are processed in real-time by Google Gemini AI.
  • Ephemeral Chats: We do not store, log, or save your chat history. Once you exit the chat, data is permanently deleted.
  • No Training: Your conversations are not used to train AI models.

4. Age Restriction

Alters.ai is strictly for users who are 18 years of age or older.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted providers to run the App:

  • Supabase: Authentication services.
  • Google Gemini: AI processing.
  • Google AdMob & Firebase: Ads and analytics.
  • Apple & Adapty: Subscription management.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your data. You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the App settings.
