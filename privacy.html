Alters.ai ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this policy in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Law on Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). 1. Data Controller The data controller for Alters.ai is the Alters.ai Team. For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at: thatsmyjobemailf@gmail.com. 2. Information We Collect We prioritize data minimization and do not collect personal information unless necessary for the App's functionality. Authentication Data: We offer Anonymous Login (using your Device ID) and Sign in with Apple. If you use Apple Auth, we receive a unique identifier and, depending on your choice, your email address. Usage Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous data about how users interact with the App (e.g., screen views, button clicks). This data is not linked to your identity. Subscription & Payments: We use Adapty to manage subscriptions. All financial transactions are processed by Apple. We do not have access to your credit card details. Advertising: We use Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID (IDFA/AAID) to serve personalized ads, subject to your consent (ATT on iOS). 3. Zero-Persistence Messaging Policy Alters.ai provides an AI-driven mentorship experience. No Storage: Your messages are processed in real-time by Google Gemini AI. Ephemeral Chats: We do not store, log, or save your chat history on our servers. Once you leave the chat session, the data is permanently deleted from our temporary processing memory. No Training: Your conversations are not used to train our AI models or for advertising purposes. 4. Age Restriction Alters.ai is intended strictly for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18. 5. Third-Party Services Your data may be processed by the following third-party providers: Supabase: For authentication services. Google Gemini (AI): To generate responses. Google AdMob & Firebase: For ads and analytics. Adapty & Apple: For subscription management. 6. Your Rights (GDPR/KVKK) Depending on your location, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we do not store chat logs, most "data requests" will involve your account ID or subscription status.
